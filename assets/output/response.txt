Great question! For effective saving, set SMART goals—Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound.

Start with short-term goals like building an emergency fund (3–6 months of expenses) or saving for a vacation. Then aim for medium-term goals such as saving for a down payment on a car or home. Finally, work toward long-term goals like retirement or your child’s education.

Also, automate your savings so money gets set aside before you even see it. Track spending to find where cuts can help, and reward progress to stay motivated. What kind of goals interest you most?
